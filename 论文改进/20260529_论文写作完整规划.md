## 0. Claude 执行原则

本文件是后续写作的主指导文件。Claude 执行论文写作、图表整理、结果解读、Related Work 组织时，必须遵守以下原则：

1. 不再沿用“OCS 是主力、图像只是辅助、fusion 一定最优”的旧叙事。

3. 必须把该结果解释为“理想图像条件下的上限实验”，不能写成真实场景性能承诺。

8. 没有真实光学观测验证，必须诚实声明本文是 physically consistent simulation and controlled inversion study。

## 1. 顶刊审稿式总体诊断

### 1.1 当前项目能否成文

可以成文。项目已经具备 SCI 二区论文的基础：

```text
真实卫星 STL 几何
-> 非均匀材料分区
-> GGX/Cook-Torrance BRDF
-> 解析射线自遮挡
-> 多观测几何 OCS 扫描
-> 光度图像渲染

-> 公平消融、random split、BRDF 敏感性、遮挡分析、噪声鲁棒性、roll 敏感性、ResNet 问题排查
```

论文不应写成“仿真程序设计”，而应写成：

> A physically consistent simulation and controlled inversion study that reveals when OCS and photometric images provide complementary attitude constraints under ideal and degraded observation conditions.

中文定位：

> 基于统一 BRDF 与自遮挡建模的 OCS-光度图像姿态反演基准研究，重点分析理想图像上限、图像退化脆弱性与 OCS 鲁棒互补价值。

### 1.2 最大优势

1. 有完整物理链条，而不是单一 CNN 或单一仿真图展示。
2. OCS 和图像共享同一套几何、姿态、材料、BRDF 与遮挡，物理一致性强。

4. ResNet 问题已转化为论文发现：强 CNN 在 clean synthetic image 上极强，但对轻微图像噪声极脆弱。
5. OCS 的鲁棒性和 fusion 的尾部改善已经有量化证据。

### 1.3 最大短板

| 短板 | 严重性 | 当前处理 |

## 2. 论文主线与核心科学问题

### 2.1 旧主线废弃

不要再写：

```text
OCS 是主力传感器，图像只是辅助。
Feature fusion 是所有方法中最优。
图像单模态只有中等姿态信息。
TinyCNN image-only 代表图像反演能力。
```

这些说法已被 ResNet-18 结果更新。

### 2.2 新主线

推荐主线：

> Unified BRDF-driven OCS and photometric image simulation enables a controlled benchmark for space object attitude inversion. Clean synthetic images provide an upper-bound case where strong CNNs achieve high accuracy, whereas OCS provides robust, interpretable, and low-cost attitude constraints under degraded image conditions. Multi-modal fusion is conditionally beneficial, improving tail errors in clean settings and becoming more valuable when observations degrade.

中文主线：

> 本文建立统一 BRDF 驱动的 OCS 与光度图像仿真框架，并在受控姿态反演实验中揭示：理想干净图像下强 CNN 可达到极高精度，但该性能对图像退化高度敏感；OCS 作为低维光度量在退化条件下更鲁棒；OCS-图像融合的价值不是“永远最优”，而是随观测质量和模态信息强度变化的条件性互补。

### 2.3 核心科学问题

建议写成：

> Under nonuniform BRDF, self-occlusion, and varying observation quality, how do scalar OCS signatures and photometric images contribute to space object attitude inversion, and under what conditions does multi-modal fusion provide robust complementary constraints?

中文：

> 在非均匀 BRDF、自遮挡和观测质量变化条件下，OCS 标量光度特征与光度图像分别承载何种姿态信息？多模态融合在什么条件下能够提供鲁棒互补约束？

## 4. 贡献点重写

Introduction 末尾建议写 4 条贡献。不要写“首次”“最先进”，要写可验证贡献。

### Contribution 1: unified physical forward model

> We develop a unified BRDF-driven forward simulation framework that generates physically consistent OCS signatures and photometric images from the same STL geometry, attitude, nonuniform material assignment, GGX BRDF, and self-occlusion model.

### Contribution 2: controlled attitude inversion benchmark

> We construct a controlled yaw-pitch attitude inversion benchmark and systematically compare OCS-only, image-only, late-fusion, and feature-fusion models under different OCS information levels and image model capacities.

### Contribution 3: clean-image upper bound and fragility

> We show that high-capacity image models can achieve very high accuracy under clean synthetic images, but this clean-image performance should be interpreted as an upper-bound case and is highly fragile under realistic image degradation.

支撑数据：

```text
ResNet image-only clean: 1.69 ± 0.07 deg, Hit@5 = 97.6%
1% Gaussian image noise: 85.85 deg, Hit@5 = 2.2%
```

### Contribution 4: robust OCS and conditional fusion

> We demonstrate that OCS provides robust, interpretable, and low-cost photometric constraints, while OCS-image fusion improves tail errors in clean settings and becomes increasingly valuable under degraded observation conditions.

支撑数据：

```text
ResNet + concat5 per_part_log: 1.47 ± 0.07 deg
ResNet image-only: 1.69 ± 0.07 deg
worst-case: 9.9 deg -> 6.6 deg
OCS MLP unaffected by image noise: 5.91 deg baseline
OCS-noise fusion gain increases from +1.97 deg to +6.29 deg as OCS noise rises from 0% to 20%
```

## 5. 关键结果的正确叙事方式

### 5.1 Clean synthetic image 不是真实场景承诺

必须写清楚：

```text
The clean-image result represents an upper-bound condition for image-based inversion, not a direct estimate of field performance.
```

建议正文解释：

> The clean rendered images are free from atmospheric turbulence, tracking error, sensor noise, PSF variation, compression artifacts, saturation, and background contamination. Therefore, the ResNet result should be interpreted as the upper-bound capability of image-based inversion under idealized photometric imagery.

### 5.2 现实中很难获得 clean image

Discussion 必须列出现实退化来源：

```text
atmospheric seeing
tracking error
sensor readout noise
background stars
stray light
low signal-to-noise ratio
PSF variation
limited angular resolution
illumination and phase-angle changes
BRDF mismatch and material aging
```

### 5.3 OCS 的价值

不要说 OCS 在 clean image 下最强。要说：

```text
OCS is not the performance upper bound when high-quality resolved photometric images are available. Its value lies in robustness, interpretability, low acquisition cost, and multi-geometry availability.
```

中文：

> 当理想高质量图像可用时，OCS 不是性能上限来源；OCS 的价值在于低成本、可解释、多观测几何以及对图像退化不敏感。

### 5.4 Fusion 的价值

不要说 fusion 永远最好。要说：

```text
Fusion provides conditional benefits: it improves tail errors under clean images and provides robustness when one modality is degraded.
```

## 10. Discussion 必须回答的问题

Discussion 不要只重复结果，必须回答审稿人会问的问题。

### 10.1 为什么 clean image 下 ResNet 这么强

建议解释：

> Clean synthetic photometric images preserve stable shape, shadow, centroid, and brightness distribution cues that are strongly correlated with yaw-pitch attitude. A high-capacity CNN can exploit these cues effectively.

但必须紧接着写：

> This does not imply that image-only inversion will remain equally accurate under field observations, where these cues are degraded by atmosphere, sensor noise, tracking errors, and changing illumination.

### 10.2 为什么 OCS 仍然重要

要点：

```text
OCS is cheaper to acquire than high-quality resolved images.
OCS can be measured under lower spatial resolution.
Multiple OCS observations across geometries are realistic for photometry.
OCS is less sensitive to image-level artifacts.
OCS is physically interpretable.
```

### 10.3 为什么 fusion 不是简单追求最低 mean

要点：

```text
Mean error is not the only criterion.
Tail error, Hit@5, robustness under degradation, and operational reliability matter.
Fusion improves worst-case and Hit@5 under clean images.
Fusion becomes more important when image or OCS quality degrades.
```

### 10.4 没有真实光学数据如何表述

建议英文：

```text
The present study focuses on physically consistent simulation and controlled inversion experiments. Real telescope observations with known attitude ground truth are difficult to obtain and are not used in this work. Therefore, the reported image-only performance should not be interpreted as field performance. Instead, the credibility of the framework is supported by analytical occlusion validation, rendering-based consistency checks, controlled degradation tests, BRDF sensitivity analysis, and comparison with trends reported in satellite photometry studies.
```

## 11. Limitations 写法

必须诚实，但不能自毁。

### 11.1 必写 limitations

```text
1. No real optical telescope images with known attitude ground truth are used.
2. The image-only clean performance is an upper-bound result under ideal rendering.
3. The current task estimates yaw and pitch under fixed roll.
4. Only one rendered image phase is used for the main image branch.
5. Atmosphere, detector response, PSF, earthshine, and background contamination are not explicitly modeled.
6. Material parameters are nominal and require future calibration.
```

### 11.2 不要这样写

```text
Our method is directly applicable to real telescope images.
The ResNet result demonstrates real-world image-based attitude accuracy.
Fusion always outperforms all single modalities.
```

## 19. 2026-06-03 主线升级补记：融合机制诊断与鲁棒融合

### 19.1 为什么这是主线升级

实验11（`resnet_fusion_robustness/run_20260603_204854`）给出了一个比原预期更重要的负面结果：

```text
ResNet-fusion clean: 1.47 ± 0.07°, Hit@5 = 99.7%
ResNet-fusion noise σ=0.01: 73.36 ± 5.07°, Hit@5 = 2.8%
ResNet-fusion noise σ=0.10: 73.57 ± 4.33°, Hit@5 = 3.3%
OCS-only clean reference: 5.91°
```

也就是说，当前 feature-level fusion 在图像噪声下没有回退到干净 OCS，而是接近 image-only 崩溃曲线。这说明“融合”不是天然鲁棒机制；融合是否有用取决于训练分布、结构设计和是否显式允许模态失效时切换权重。

论文主线因此从：

```text
OCS-image fusion is conditionally beneficial.
```

升级为：

```text
OCS-image fusion is architecture- and training-dependent. Naive feature-level fusion can become image-dominant and fail under image degradation; OCS can act as a fallback constraint only when the fusion design explicitly supports degradation awareness, modality dropout, adaptive gating, uncertainty weighting, or OCS-anchored prediction.
```

中文写作定位同步升级为：

> 本文不仅比较 OCS、图像和融合的精度，还系统揭示融合什么时候有用、什么时候失效、为什么失效；进一步检验是否能通过训练策略和结构设计让 OCS 在图像退化时真正“托住”融合模型。

### 19.2 实验12：融合主导性诊断与鲁棒融合升级

实验12进入主线调整，不作为可有可无的 future work。它的目标不是简单刷分，而是回答三个机制问题：

1. 当前 feature fusion 是否确实学习到图像主导？
2. OCS 分支在图像退化时是没有信息，还是有信息但 fusion head 不会切换？
3. 通过退化增强、模态 dropout、门控/不确定性加权或 OCS-anchored 结构，是否能让融合在图像失效时回退到 OCS 约 5.91° 的量级？

必须包含的诊断实验：

| 诊断 | 判据 | 论文用途 |
|---|---|---|
| 分支遮蔽 / 均值替换 | 遮蔽图像分支若崩溃、遮蔽 OCS 分支若变化小，则说明图像主导 | 证明当前 fusion head 的实际依赖关系 |
| 图像退化 + 图像遮蔽 | 若遮蔽退化图像后反而接近 OCS-only，说明 OCS 信息存在但融合策略不会切换 | 区分“OCS 无效”和“融合机制无效” |
| 梯度 / 权重贡献分析 | 比较 image feature 与 OCS feature 对 fusion 输出的梯度范数或首层权重范数 | 给出机制证据，不只看结果 |
| 双向扰动敏感性 | 固定 OCS 扫图像噪声；固定图像扫 OCS 噪声 | 支撑不对称鲁棒性结论 |

可执行的升级方案按优先级排序：

| 方案 | 核心做法 | 成功判据 |
|---|---|---|
| 图像退化增强 | 训练时加入 clean + Gaussian noise + brightness，可选 blur/downsample | 图像噪声测试不再 73° 崩溃 |
| 模态 dropout | 训练时随机屏蔽图像分支或 OCS 分支，强迫任一模态单独可用 | 遮蔽/退化时仍能回退到可解释单模态 |
| OCS-anchored residual fusion | 先由 OCS 预测基准姿态，图像只预测门控残差 `y = normalize(y_ocs + g·Δ_image)` | 图像失效时 gate 变小，性能接近 OCS-only |
| adaptive gating / uncertainty late fusion | 分别训练 image 与 OCS 预测器，学习置信度或反方差加权 | 干净图像下保持低误差，噪声图像下权重转向 OCS |

建议评价矩阵：

```text
clean
image Gaussian noise σ=0.01 / 0.10
brightness ×0.50 / ×1.50
OCS noise 0 / 1 / 5 / 10 / 20%
可选：图像与 OCS 同时退化
```

主要指标：

```text
mean angular error
Hit@5°
p90/p95 或 worst-case（若脚本已有）
分支遮蔽后的 mean / Hit@5°
图像分支权重或 gate / uncertainty 统计
```

### 19.3 成功与失败路径都能服务论文

成功路径：

> 如果模态 dropout、退化增强、门控或 OCS-anchored fusion 能把图像噪声下的 fusion 从 73° 显著拉回，例如接近 OCS-only 5.91° 或至少低于 15°，则论文主线进一步增强：统一框架不仅发现 naive fusion 的失效机制，还验证了让 OCS 作为 fallback constraint 的可行训练/结构条件。

失败路径：

> 如果上述方案仍无法稳定改善，不能硬写成成功。应把结果作为更强的机制性发现：在 clean image 远强于 OCS 的数据分布下，简单 feature fusion、普通增强甚至初级 dropout 都不保证 bidirectional robustness；要实现可靠 fallback，需要显式不确定性建模、部署时质量估计或更强的结构约束。这仍然能支撑本文标题中的 “when fusion helps and fails”，只是结论从“提出鲁棒融合方案”转为“揭示鲁棒融合的必要设计条件和失败边界”。

无论结果成功或失败，写作红线不变：

```text
不写 fusion automatically robust。
不写 OCS 一定能托住任何融合模型。
不把负面结果隐藏。
不把新模型包装成真实场景 SOTA。
```

```text

| bright×1.50 | 2.00±0.22° | 97.4% | 3.62° | 98.97° |

fully robust fusion
U1 已证明 OCS fallback
image masking 后接近 OCS-only
```

> Naive feature fusion does not automatically provide fallback under dominant-modality failure. However, the present results do not isolate OCS as the sole cause of the recovered robustness; they show that degradation-aware training prevents the fused representation from over-specializing to pristine image statistics.
