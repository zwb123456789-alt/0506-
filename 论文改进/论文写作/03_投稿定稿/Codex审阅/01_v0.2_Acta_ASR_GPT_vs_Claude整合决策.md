## 4. 红线结论

最终 v0.2 不得出现以下结论：

- fusion automatically robust。
- U1 automatically switches to OCS。
- OCS standalone fallback。
- near-perfect / fully robust。
- real telescope validation has been achieved。
- operational robustness / field-proven robustness。
- phase120 generalization is solved。
- obs-aug is a successful general robust training strategy。
- 12f best beta is a deployable automatic gate。

最终 v0.2 必须出现以下边界：

- Clean rendered images are idealized upper-bound conditions.
- Naive clean-trained feature fusion is image-dominant and fails under image noise.
- U1 is degradation-aware OCS-image co-utilization, not standalone fallback.
- Severe combined degradation, phase120, and rare polar outliers remain failure modes.
- The benchmark is simulation-based and does not use real optical telescope images with known attitude ground truth.
