## 2. 可进入 v0.2 的核心结果

### 2.1 Naive feature fusion 的失败机制

| 条件 | normal | image masked | OCS masked |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| clean | 1.57° | 52.84° | 18.14° |
| image noise σ=0.01 | 75.08° | 52.84° | 88.88° |
| image noise σ=0.10 | 72.48° | 52.84° | 89.96° |

v0.2 可写：

```text
Clean-trained feature fusion is image-dominant under nominal conditions.
When image noise is introduced, the degraded image branch actively contaminates the fused representation.
The OCS branch still carries useful information, because removing it worsens the noisy fusion from ~75° to ~89°.
However, the fusion head has not learned an OCS-standalone fallback: image masking improves the noisy case to ~53°, still far from the dedicated OCS-only predictor at 5.91°.
```

禁止写：

```text
image masking makes fusion close to OCS-only.
OCS automatically saves naive fusion.
```

## 3. 对论文主线的修正

旧升级主线：

```text
OCS can act as a fallback constraint when fusion design supports degradation awareness.
```

根据实验12，应细化为：

```text
Naive feature fusion does not automatically provide fallback under dominant-modality failure. In the tested degradation set, exposing the fusion model to image degradations during training restores strong mean and p90 robustness, whereas modality dropout alone fails. However, the present results do not isolate OCS as the sole cause of the recovered robustness; they show that degradation-aware training prevents the fused representation from over-specializing to pristine image statistics.
```

中文主线：

> 实验12进一步表明，融合失效并非简单“OCS 无信息”，而是 clean-trained fusion head 未学会在图像失效时把 OCS 当作独立 fallback 使用。图像退化增强能显著修复已测试退化下的均值和 p90 崩溃，但现有证据只能证明退化感知训练有效，不能单独证明恢复完全来自 OCS 托底。
