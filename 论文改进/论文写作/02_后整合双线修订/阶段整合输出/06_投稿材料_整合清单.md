## 5. Cover Letter 安全口径

当前只保留要点，不生成最终信件。

### 5.1 可以写入的要点

1. The manuscript presents a controlled simulation benchmark for yaw-pitch attitude inversion using paired OCS signatures and clean rendered photometric images.
2. Both modalities are generated under shared geometry, BRDF, attitude, observation-geometry and visibility assumptions.
3. The study separates clean-image upper-bound performance from controlled degradation stress tests.
4. High-capacity image models achieve strong accuracy under clean rendered images, while image-only performance can be fragile under controlled image degradation.
5. Practical component-level OCS features provide an interpretable scalar photometric constraint.
6. OCS-image fusion is framed as conditional complementarity, especially through tail-error reduction and modality-dependent failure modes.
7. The manuscript explicitly states that real optical telescope validation, calibrated material measurements and full atmosphere/sensor modeling remain outside the present study.
8. The work should be positioned as a methods/benchmark study, not an operational deployment report.

### 5.2 禁用表述

1. `first`、`state-of-the-art`、`breakthrough`、`validated in real observations`。
2. `field performance`、`operationally validated`、`ready for deployment`。
3. clean rendered images 代表 real telescope images。
4. controlled Gaussian noise / brightness / OCS-noise 是完整 atmosphere/sensor model。
5. fusion is universally superior 或 always improves performance。
6. `all_raw` 是 operational OCS feature。
7. `r = 0.003` 是 ResNet-pair evidence。
8. 在目标期刊未确认前绑定具体编辑偏好、格式或期刊要求。
