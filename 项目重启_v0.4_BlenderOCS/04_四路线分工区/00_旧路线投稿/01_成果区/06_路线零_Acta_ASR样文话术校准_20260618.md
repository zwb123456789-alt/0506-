## 2. 对路线零的总体判断

路线零直接投 v0.3 是可行的，但主稿应主动把自己写成：

```text
a controlled simulation benchmark for known-object optical attitude inversion,
not an operational unknown-target attitude-estimation system.
```

样文共同特征不是“把仿真包装成真实验证”，而是把仿真的边界说清楚：

- Acta 样文强调 image/dataset generation、rendering assumptions、validation and representativeness。
- ASR 样文强调 RSO characterization、light curve / photometry、known shape or known optical assumptions、ambiguity and future real-data use。
- 四篇样文都允许使用 synthetic / simulated data，但会期待作者说明 known inputs、simplifications、validation target、future real-data gap。

因此，路线零应保留目前工作稿中的 `controlled benchmark`、`internally consistent v0.3`、`clean-image upper-bound`、`no field-performance claim` 口径，不宜再回到“统一物理真实性”“工程可用系统”一类强说法。

## 3. Acta Astronautica 优先话术

Acta 优先投稿时，主稿标题、摘要、Introduction 和 Cover Letter 建议突出以下关键词：

```text
controlled benchmark
dataset generation and validation
synthetic photometric images
shared forward model
non-cooperative / uncooperative spacecraft
monocular / optical pose or attitude estimation
training, validation and testing
assumptions and simplifications
representativeness rather than field performance
```

推荐表达：

- `We develop a controlled simulation benchmark for optical attitude inversion of a known, non-cooperative spacecraft model.`
- `Both OCS signatures and photometric images are generated under a shared v0.3 forward-model setting.`
- `The benchmark is designed to compare modality information under consistent assumptions, rather than to claim field-ready performance.`
- `Assumptions and simplifications are stated explicitly, following the style of synthetic dataset and rendering-validation studies in Acta Astronautica.`
- `The clean rendered-image result is reported as an idealized upper-bound condition.`

Acta 侧重点应放在：

- 为什么同一前向模型下比较 OCS 与图像是一个有用 benchmark。
- 为什么 face-center / facet-level OCS 在 v0.3 内部是自洽的。
- 为什么 degradation-aware fusion 的结论是“机制诊断”，不是“真实观测鲁棒性证明”。
- 图表应服务于 pipeline、dataset split、main benchmark、degradation、ablation、limitations。

## 4. ASR 备选话术

若转投 Advances in Space Research，建议把摘要和 Introduction 的前半部分更靠近 ASR 语境：

```text
resident space object (RSO)
space situational awareness (SSA)
light curve / photometric signature
attitude and optical-property interpretation
known object shape
BRDF-driven photometry
ambiguity and non-uniqueness
simulated light curves / simulated observations
```

推荐表达：

- `The study is motivated by RSO characterization in SSA scenarios where optical brightness signatures provide attitude-related information.`
- `OCS is treated as a light-curve-like scalar photometric constraint generated from a known object model.`
- `The inversion task is evaluated under controlled simulated observations with known true attitudes.`
- `The results should be interpreted as a benchmark for observability and modality complementarity, not as an operational SSA pipeline.`
- `Known-shape and nominal-material assumptions are necessary for the present inversion setting and define the scope of the results.`

ASR 侧重点应放在：

- RSO 光度特征为什么能提供姿态相关信息。
- OCS 与 light curve / photometry 的关系。
- known-shape、BRDF、observation geometry、attitude ambiguity 的边界。
- 真实 SSA 应用仍需真实光度、材料标定、传感器/大气模型和姿态真值。

## 5. 当前工作稿应保留的好口径

工作稿中已经比较接近样文接受口径的部分：

- 摘要中写明 `controlled benchmark` 和 `clean rendered images`。
- Introduction 中说明“同一前向模型下比较模态信息”，这很像 Acta 的 dataset / framework 文章逻辑。
- Methods 3.1 已明确 `simulation-focused`、`no real optical telescope images`、`v0.3 face-center / facet-level approximation`。
- Discussion 5.6 已把 limitations 独立列出，审稿人会更容易接受。
- Conclusion 已把未来工作限制在 calibrated materials、broader phase and roll、sensor/atmosphere、real optical observations。

这些内容不建议删除；后续只需要再压缩和期刊化，不需要推倒重写。

## 6. 建议小修点

### 6.1 Abstract

建议压缩一句过长的技术细节，把“贡献链”写得更像样文：

```text
This paper presents a controlled v0.3 benchmark that links OCS signatures and photometric images through a shared BRDF-driven forward model, enabling a consistent comparison of OCS-only, image-only and fusion-based attitude inversion under clean and degraded synthetic observations.
```

同时保留但稍后置：

```text
The OCS branch uses the v0.3 face-center / facet-level approximation and is not the later v0.4 Blender-derived pixel-level OCS implementation.
```

这句话不一定要放在摘要前半段；可以放到 Methods / Scope note 中更自然。摘要里可保留一句短版：

```text
The benchmark is restricted to the internally consistent v0.3 face-center OCS formulation.
```

### 6.2 Introduction

建议将第一段更靠近 Acta / ASR 共同语境：

```text
Optical measurements are increasingly used to characterize non-cooperative spacecraft and resident space objects when cooperative telemetry is unavailable.
```

然后再引出：

```text
However, scalar photometric signatures and resolved photometric images are often simulated, validated and inverted under different assumptions.
```

这比一上来强调“姿态反演难”更像样文的叙述方式。

### 6.3 Related Work

建议把 Related Work 小标题微调为：

- `Optical signatures and BRDF-based RSO characterization`
- `Light-curve / OCS-based attitude inversion`
- `Synthetic image generation and image-based spacecraft pose estimation`
- `Fusion and robustness under degraded observations`

这样同时兼容 Acta 和 ASR。

### 6.4 Methods

建议保留当前的 scope note，但把“不是 v0.4”尽量写成作者边界，而不是防御性声明：

```text
All OCS results in this route-zero manuscript follow the v0.3 face-center / facet-level approximation. This choice is kept fixed throughout the benchmark to preserve internal consistency between OCS features, rendered images and inversion experiments.
```

然后另起一句：

```text
The later v0.4 Blender-derived pixel-level OCS chain is outside the scope of this independent legacy submission.
```

### 6.5 Results

Acta / ASR 样文都很重视结果边界。建议每个强结果后加一句“interpretation”：

- ResNet clean image：`idealized upper-bound condition`
- OCS-only：`interpretable scalar photometric constraint`
- Fusion：`architecture- and training-dependent`
- U1：`stable under tested synthetic degradations, not real-observation proof`

这会让审稿人觉得你在主动控制 claim，而不是夸大。

### 6.6 Limitations / Conclusion

当前 Limitations 足够好，但可以更期刊化地压成四类：

| 类别 | 建议表述 |
|---|---|
| Data | no real telescope images with known attitude ground truth |
| Task | yaw-pitch only, fixed roll |
| Forward model | nominal material parameters and v0.3 face-center OCS |
| Observation chain | no calibrated atmosphere, sensor, PSF, earthshine or background model |

Conclusion 最后一段建议保留“future work”而不是“defect list”。样文普遍把限制转成 future work。

## 7. Cover Letter 可用话术

### 7.1 Acta Astronautica

```text
Dear Editor,

We submit this manuscript as a full-length research article for Acta Astronautica. The paper presents a controlled BRDF-driven simulation benchmark for attitude inversion of a known non-cooperative spacecraft model from two optical modalities: scalar OCS signatures and resolved photometric images. The study is motivated by the need to compare image-based and photometric attitude cues under consistent forward-model assumptions.

The main contribution is not a claim of field-ready operational performance. Rather, the manuscript establishes an internally consistent v0.3 benchmark, evaluates OCS-only, image-only and fusion-based inversion under clean and degraded synthetic observations, and diagnoses when fusion provides robustness or inherits image degradation. The scope, assumptions and simplifications are stated explicitly, including the use of a v0.3 face-center OCS formulation, nominal material parameters, fixed-roll yaw-pitch attitude estimation, and the absence of real telescope imagery.

We believe the manuscript fits Acta Astronautica because it contributes to optical simulation, synthetic benchmark generation, non-cooperative spacecraft pose/attitude estimation, and robustness analysis for space applications.
```

### 7.2 Advances in Space Research

```text
Dear Editor,

We submit this manuscript as a research article for Advances in Space Research. The work addresses optical characterization of resident space objects by studying how scalar photometric signatures and resolved photometric images contribute to attitude inversion under a shared BRDF-driven forward model.

The manuscript frames OCS as a light-curve-like photometric constraint for a known object model and compares it with image-based and fusion-based inversion under controlled simulated observations. The results clarify the observability and complementarity of the two optical modalities, while explicitly separating clean synthetic upper-bound performance from degraded-observation robustness and future real-data requirements.

The paper is simulation-focused and does not claim operational SSA deployment. It identifies the assumptions needed for the present benchmark and motivates future work on calibrated materials, broader viewing geometries, sensor/atmosphere modeling, and real optical observations with reliable attitude truth.
```

## 8. 禁用或慎用表达

以下表达不建议出现在路线零主稿、cover letter 或投稿摘要中：

| 不建议表达 | 替代表达 |
|---|---|
| field-proven | controlled / simulation-validated / benchmarked |
| operational-ready | potential relevance to future SSA workflows |
| real telescope validation | future real optical validation is required |
| full 3-DOF attitude inversion | yaw-pitch attitude inversion under fixed roll |
| physically consistent OCS-image framework | internally consistent v0.3 OCS-image benchmark |
| unified OCS model | shared v0.3 forward-model setting |
| automatic fallback to OCS | degradation-aware co-utilization of OCS and image cues |
| robust under real observation | robust under tested synthetic degradations |
| unknown-target attitude estimation | known-object / model-known attitude inversion |
| v0.4 Blender-derived OCS | v0.3 face-center / facet-level OCS approximation |
